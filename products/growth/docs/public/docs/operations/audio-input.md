# Audio input

The Growth assistant currently accepts text. Its former installation-based audio upload,
transcription and website-widget audio endpoints are removed. A recorded message in the human
Inbox remains a provider attachment; receiving it does not start an agent or transcription task.

Customer channel and widget automation belongs to the standalone Agents product. Audio support
must be configured and verified there before offering it to customers. Growth does not proxy
that product's media endpoints or credentials.

Normal Growth media uploads, publishing and staff Inbox attachments remain available through
their existing product controls.
