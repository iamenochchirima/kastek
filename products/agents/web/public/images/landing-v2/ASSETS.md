# Landing v2 artwork

`coast.webp` and `coast-small.webp` are 2048 × 768 and 960 × 360 encodings of
coastal artwork generated for the owner-approved Agents landing concept on
28 October 2026. The image is illustrative, not a photograph of an identified
location or customer. It has no embedded product interfaces or text.

The coastline is reused inside the guesthouse website illustration. All product
interfaces are rendered in HTML. Google provider logos use the existing assets
and attribution in `../integrations/BRAND-ASSETS.md`.
