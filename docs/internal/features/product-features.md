# Product Features

This is the active catalogue of customer-facing Kasitek capabilities. It answers **what each product does** and its current status.

The [Product Roadmap](../roadmap/product-roadmap.md) owns delivery timing. The [Technical Blueprint](../architecture/kasitek-technical-blueprint.md) owns technical design and system boundaries.

## Status Definitions

- `Live`: available for real customer use.
- `In Progress`: actively being built or connected.
- `Planned`: accepted product direction, not yet started.
- `Research`: valuable direction still being validated.

## Feature Governance

- Add a feature here only after it is accepted as part of the Kasitek offering.
- Change its status here when its customer availability changes.
- Put timing, targets, and release sequencing in the Product Roadmap.
- Put technical designs, data ownership, protocols, and controls in the Technical Blueprint.
- Keep future product ideas in the roadmap until they become accepted products.
