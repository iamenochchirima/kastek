**Mission**  
Kasitek Labs builds AI-powered products and services that help SMBs manage customer conversations, outbound campaigns, social content, and business automation.

**Vision**  
To build a practical growth and operations company for SMBs, anchored by Kasitek as the core product for communication, campaigns, content, and automation.

**Company Structure**  
Kasitek Labs is the parent company. The business is built around AI-powered products and services for SMBs, with Kasitek as the core product platform and individual product lines focused on specific customer workflows. The business operates across two revenue streams:

- product revenue from recurring subscriptions to Kasitek and product lines such as KasiAgents, KasiReach, and KasiSocial
- services revenue from implementation, customisation, managed automation, integrations, and white-label partnerships built on Kasitek infrastructure

## Positioning And Target Market

### Positioning Statement

Kasitek Labs builds AI-powered products and services for SMBs, anchored by Kasitek, its growth and operations product for customer conversations, outbound campaigns, social content, and business automation.

### Ideal Customer Profile

#### Primary: SMB Owner

- business size: 1 to 50 employees
- industries: medical aesthetics, hospitality, food service, retail, transport, professional services
- location: South Africa first, especially Durban, Cape Town, and Johannesburg, with later SADC expansion
- tech profile: not technical, usually relying on WhatsApp groups, spreadsheets, or manual processes
- core pain: missed messages, manual follow-up, inconsistent marketing
- trigger to buy: wants growth and is tired of manual operations

#### Secondary: Individual Creator / Solopreneur

- uses KasiSocial for scheduling and AI-assisted content
- manages several social platforms without a team
- natural upsell path into KasiAgents or KasiReach as the business grows

#### Partner Channel: White-Label Agencies

- digital marketing agencies serving SMBs
- resellers deploying Kasitek products under their own brand
- Kasitek provides infrastructure while the partner owns the client relationship

## Product Suite

Kasitek Labs operates a product suite under the Kasitek product platform. The products work better together but can also be sold independently.

### KasiAgents

**Positioning**  
AI agents that handle customer conversations so businesses never miss a lead.

**What it does**

- deploys AI agents on WhatsApp, website chat, Facebook, Instagram, and voice channels
- answers questions
- qualifies leads
- books appointments
- sends documents
- escalates to humans when needed

**Target buyer**

- SMBs receiving customer enquiries via messaging
- especially clinics, salons, restaurants, transport businesses, and retailers
- strongest fit: businesses handling 20 or more inbound messages per day manually

**Channels covered**

- WhatsApp Business API
- website widget
- Facebook Messenger
- Instagram DM
- voice via Retell AI and Telnyx
- SMS

**Why not Meta Business Agent**

Meta Business Agent covers WhatsApp, Messenger, and Instagram inbound. KasiAgents adds voice, website chat, SMS, deep CRM integration, custom multi-agent flows, and an implementation and management layer.

### KasiReach

**Positioning**  
Outbound campaigns that reach customers before they reach the business.

**What it does**

- bulk outbound campaigns via WhatsApp, email, and SMS
- segmented messaging
- scheduled sends
- campaign analytics
- automated follow-up sequences

**Target buyer**

- SMBs with an existing customer base they want to market to repeatedly
- examples: restaurants, clinics, retailers

**Channels covered**

- WhatsApp broadcast
- email
- SMS

**Why not Meta Business Agent**

Meta Business Agent is reactive. KasiReach is proactive and campaign-driven, built to send to a business-owned customer list on schedule.

### KasiSocial

**Positioning**  
Plan, create, and schedule social content across multiple platforms with AI assistance.

**What it does**

- AI-assisted social media content creation
- scheduling across multiple platforms simultaneously
- central management of brand social presence from one dashboard

**Target buyer**

- SMB owners and solopreneurs managing their own social media
- individual creators running a personal brand

**Channels covered**

- Facebook
- Instagram
- LinkedIn
- X
- TikTok on the roadmap

**Why not Meta Business Agent**

Meta Business Agent does not offer content creation or scheduling. KasiSocial also covers non-Meta platforms Meta will never support.

## Go-To-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Foundation

**Timeline**  
June to August 2026

**Focus**  
Close the first five paying KasiAgents clients and turn the first implementations into case studies.

**Actions**

- direct outbound via WhatsApp and LinkedIn DM to Durban SMB owners
- demo-first sales process
- referral activation from existing clients
- Upwork and Fiverr Pro for implementation projects

### Phase 2: Channel

**Timeline**  
September to November 2026

**Focus**  
Launch white-label partnerships and begin KasiReach and KasiSocial beta programs.

**Actions**

- agency outreach to firms like Ruby Digital and Tomorrow Labs
- partner deck and commercial terms
- KasiReach beta with existing clients
- KasiSocial beta with 5 to 10 solopreneurs

### Phase 3: Scale

**Timeline**  
December 2026 to June 2027

**Focus**  
Reach 20 or more recurring clients, activate white-label partners, and put all three products in market.

**Actions**

- LinkedIn and Facebook paid advertising
- content-led inbound
- SADC expansion through remote sales and referral partners
- target R50,000 MRR by June 2027
