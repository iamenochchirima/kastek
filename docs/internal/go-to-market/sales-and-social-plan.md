## Social Media Content Plan - 2026

### Overview

Kasitek Labs' social media strategy is designed to build Enoch's personal brand around practical AI systems for SMBs while generating inbound interest in Kasitek products and services. LinkedIn is the primary platform. The strategy is founder-led, technical, and tied to real builds.

### Platforms And Priority

| Platform | Priority | Audience | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | Primary | Agency owners, SMB decision-makers, tech founders | Thought leadership leading to demos and partnerships |
| Facebook | Secondary | Durban and SA SMB owners, local business communities | Community presence, social proof, retargeting |
| Instagram | Secondary | Visual SMB owners in hospitality, aesthetics, and food | Brand aesthetic, product visuals, KasiSocial case studies |
| X | Tertiary | Tech and AI community, developers, startup ecosystem | Build in public, AI commentary, technical positioning |
| WhatsApp Status | Local | Existing network, warm leads, local business owners | Soft nurture and direct conversion |

### Content Pillars

| Pillar | Name | Share | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Build in Public | 25% | What was deployed this week, lessons from real builds |
| P2 | AI Education for SMBs | 25% | Explaining what AI automation can and cannot do for SMBs |
| P3 | Client Results and Social Proof | 20% | Response time improvements, client setups, before/after |
| P4 | African Tech Perspective | 15% | Local regulatory, operational, and market perspective |
| P5 | Kasitek Product Showcases | 15% | Demos, feature launches, onboarding walkthroughs |

### Weekly Posting Cadence

| Day | Platform | Format | Pillar |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | LinkedIn | Text insight or observation | AI Education |
| Tuesday | Instagram + Facebook | Visual result or product screenshot | Social Proof |
| Wednesday | LinkedIn | Long-form build-in-public story | Build in Public |
| Thursday | X | Short AI commentary | African Tech Perspective |
| Friday | LinkedIn + Facebook | Product showcase or weekly wrap-up | Kasitek Product |
| Daily | WhatsApp Status | Casual behind-the-scenes updates | Mixed |

### Monthly Content Formula

| Content Type | Count per month |
| --- | --- |
| Build in public / behind-the-scenes | 5 |
| Educational AI content for SMBs | 5 |
| Client results / case studies | 4 |
| Product showcases / demos | 3 |
| African tech perspective / opinion | 3 |

### Content Creation Process

- Sunday: spend 30 minutes planning the week's five posts
- use KasiSocial to draft with AI assistance, edit in the founder voice, and schedule
- repurpose LinkedIn long-form into Instagram carousel and WhatsApp voice note
- reply to every comment within 24 hours
- review performance monthly and double down on the strongest pillar
