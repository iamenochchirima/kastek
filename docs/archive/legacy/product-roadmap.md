# Product Roadmap

Q3 2026 to Q2 2027 | Version 1.0 | June 2026

## Status Definitions

- `Live`: built and deployed in production
- `In Progress`: currently being built
- `Planned`: committed, not yet started
- `Research`: feasibility still being explored

## Future Product Portfolio

This section captures product directions that fit the Kasitek Labs thesis but are not yet committed roadmap items. These should be treated as strategic options, not delivery promises.

### Core Expansion Candidates

| Product Direction | Role In The Portfolio | Why It Fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KasiInbox | Unified communication hub for WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, web chat, email, and human handoff | All conversations eventually need one operational home |
| KasiSites | Websites and landing pages for SMBs, connected to agents, forms, campaigns, analytics, and bookings | Strong fit for services delivery and SMB demand |
| KasiBookings | Appointment scheduling, reminders, deposits, rescheduling, and no-show reduction | Natural extension for clinics, beauty, transport, consultants, and service businesses |
| KasiCRM | Lightweight CRM for contacts, leads, deals, follow-ups, pipeline, and customer history | Connects agents, campaigns, inbox, and bookings into one customer record |
| KasiVoice | Voice agents, missed-call follow-up, call summaries, call routing, and phone-based booking | Premium channel for businesses that still rely heavily on phone calls |
| KasiReviews | Review requests, Google reviews, negative feedback handling, testimonial capture, and social proof | Helps SMBs turn customer experience into reputation growth |
| KasiForms | Forms, intake flows, quote requests, lead capture, surveys, and onboarding flows | Lightweight entry point into automation and customer data capture |
| KasiPay | Payment links, deposits, invoices, payment reminders, and later subscriptions | Useful when paired with bookings, services, and follow-up workflows |
| KasiAnalytics | Unified reporting across messages, campaigns, social, agents, leads, bookings, and revenue | Starts as a platform capability and may later become its own product surface |

### White-Label And Partner Directions

| Product Direction | Role In The Portfolio | Why It Fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kasitek White Label | Agency portal for reselling Kasitek products under another brand | Supports distribution through agencies and implementation partners |
| Kasitek Services / Studio | Services arm for websites, custom agents, automations, integrations, onboarding, and managed support | Converts implementation demand into structured delivery and product learning |
| KasiTemplates | Prebuilt agent templates, campaign templates, website templates, social calendars, and industry packs | Can become a reusable asset library and marketplace over time |

### Suggested Expansion Order

The strongest near-term expansion candidate is `KasiSites`, because it connects directly to services revenue and gives many SMBs a visible entry point into the broader Kasitek system.

After that, `KasiBookings` and `KasiCRM` are the most natural product extensions because they turn conversations, campaigns, and website leads into operational workflows.
