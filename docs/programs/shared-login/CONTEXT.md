# Shared account language

Proposed terms for the shared-login program. Product relationships remain separate from a person's sign-in identity.

## Language

**KasiTek account**:
A person's shared sign-in identity across participating KasiTek products. It does not by itself grant a product subscription, role, or workspace membership.

**Product principal**:
A product's local record for an authenticated person. Product business records refer to this local identity.

**Workspace membership**:
The relationship granting a product principal a role in a specific product workspace. Membership in one product does not grant membership in another.

**Agency client**:
A customer business served by an agency. It is distinct from the people who sign in on that business's behalf.

**Staff entitlement**:
An explicit grant permitting a person to use central KasiTek administration. A customer account or workspace-owner role does not grant it.
