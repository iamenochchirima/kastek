# KasiTek Labs brand directions

Created 10 October 2026. These are visual identity studies, not approved brand guidelines.

Stitch project: https://stitch.withgoogle.com/projects/10397085124102247750

## Shared brief

KasiTek Labs is the parent software studio. Agents, Growth and Agency are distinct products in the same family:

- **Agents:** customer conversations, AI agents and human handoff.
- **Growth:** campaigns, leads and business follow-up.
- **Agency:** white-label client workspaces for agencies.

Custom software work has its own Studio section. The identity needs a readable wordmark, a small symbol, related product marks, typography, colours and a reusable visual motif. The homepage demonstrates those decisions in use.

Keep the communication plain. Do not invent clients, awards, testimonials or performance statistics. Avoid decorative numbering, sparkle icons, generic gradients, glassmorphism, pill badges and repetitive card grids. Product previews use illustrative sample data.

## Ink & Signal

Warm paper `#F3F0E9`, near-black `#20221F`, vermilion `#D9472B`. A restrained grotesk with a small serif accent. Large left-aligned typography and an original flat K construction made from bars and negative space. Product identities reuse the bar motif. Horizontal product presentations and realistic interface excerpts.

Headline: **Good ideas. Real working tools.**

## Fieldwork

Soft chalk, dark forest and acid yellow. Humanist typography paired with a restrained editorial serif. A field-notes visual language built around a simple branching K mark and contact-sheet compositions. Realistic South African studio photography grounds the identity, with software excerpts presented as work in progress.

Headline: **Built for the work at hand.**

## Common Ground

Bright white, cobalt and dark ink. A typographic identity with a square K monogram and a connected-line visual language. Spacious architectural layouts and product-specific interface compositions. A confident, precise family of software products.

Headline: **Useful software. Made to fit.**

## Review criteria

Compare the opening composition, readability, recognisability of the mark, relationship between the products, and whether the identity carries through the whole page. A generated mockup is a concept, not evidence that an interface works or a logo is legally available. Refine the chosen identity and create vector master assets before adopting it.
