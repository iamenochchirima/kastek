Product Roadmap - 2026/2027
Q3 2026 to Q2 2027 | Version 1.0 | June 2026

This roadmap covers the main Kasitek product lines, including KasiAgents, KasiReach, and KasiSocial, across four quarters. It is intended as a living document updated as market feedback comes in.

Status Definitions​

  • Live: built and deployed in production
  • In Progress: currently being built
  • Planned: committed, not yet started
  • Research: feasibility still being explored

Future Product Portfolio​

This section captures product directions that fit the Kasitek Labs thesis but are not yet committed roadmap items. These should be treated as strategic options, not delivery promises.

Core Expansion Candidates​

Product DirectionRole In The PortfolioWhy It Fits
KasiInboxUnified communication hub for WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, web chat, email, and human handoffAll conversations eventually need one operational home
KasiSitesWebsites and landing pages for SMBs, connected to agents, forms, campaigns, analytics, and bookingsStrong fit for services delivery and SMB demand
KasiBookingsAppointment scheduling, reminders, deposits, rescheduling, and no-show reductionNatural extension for clinics, beauty, transport, consultants, and service businesses
KasiCRMLightweight CRM for contacts, leads, deals, follow-ups, pipeline, and customer historyConnects agents, campaigns, inbox, and bookings into one customer record
KasiVoiceVoice agents, missed-call follow-up, call summaries, call routing, and phone-based bookingPremium channel for businesses that still rely heavily on phone calls
KasiReviewsReview requests, Google reviews, negative feedback handling, testimonial capture, and social proofHelps SMBs turn customer experience into reputation growth
KasiFormsForms, intake flows, quote requests, lead capture, surveys, and onboarding flowsLightweight entry point into automation and customer data capture
KasiPayPayment links, deposits, invoices, payment reminders, and later subscriptionsUseful when paired with bookings, services, and follow-up workflows
KasiAnalyticsUnified reporting across messages, campaigns, social, agents, leads, bookings, and revenueStarts as a platform capability and may later become its own product surface

White-Label And Partner Directions​

Product DirectionRole In The PortfolioWhy It Fits
Kasitek White LabelAgency portal for reselling Kasitek products under another brandSupports distribution through agencies and implementation partners
Kasitek Services / StudioServices arm for websites, custom agents, automations, integrations, onboarding, and managed supportConverts implementation demand into structured delivery and product learning

Suggested Expansion Order​

The strongest near-term expansion candidate is KasiSites, because it connects directly to services revenue and gives many SMBs a visible entry point into the broader Kasitek system.

After that, KasiBookings and KasiCRM are the most natural product extensions because they turn conversations, campaigns, and website leads into operational workflows.
