## 7) Franchise Data (as published)

### Headline Claims
- Over 28 years in business
- Over 25 years franchising
- 19 stores (13 franchised, 6 company owned)

### FAQ Key Facts
- **Franchise cost:** Delivery store R790,000 + VAT
- **Minimum own contribution for finance:** At least 50%
- **Finance timeline:** Around 4 months
- **Typical store size:** 80-100 square metres
- **Royalty:** 6% of turnover
- **Advertising fund:** 2% of turnover
- **Typical hours:** 8:30 am to 4:30 pm, usually closed Sundays and Bank Holidays
- **Break-even claim:** Normally 3 months
- **Gross profit claim:** 64%
- **Opening timeline after payment:** One month
- **Franchise term:** Five years, renewable if compliant

## 8) AI Agent Guidance Snippets

- If customer asks to order: route to the official order link first; then nearest store.
- If customer asks pricing: mention prices are site-crawled and can vary by branch/date.
- If customer asks menu help: start with category, then offer item examples.
- If customer asks franchise: provide published figures but advise confirmation with head office.
