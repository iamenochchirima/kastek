# Agents demo imagery

These images support illustrative product demos. The people, names, messages, orders, timestamps and activity records do not represent live customers or live backend runs.

## Studio photo

`working-studio.png` was generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-11. The original is preserved in the tool's generated-images directory; this copy is the project asset.

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Generate an editorial lifestyle photograph for a premium South African software studio website. Wide landscape 3:2 photograph, candid woman entrepreneur in her early thirties with dark brown skin and short natural hair, wearing an understated charcoal knit, seated side-on at a light oak desk using a silver laptop in a warm real design studio. Subject on the LEFT third, photographed at medium distance with authentic skin texture and relaxed focused expression, looking at laptop, not camera. Large soft daylight from left windows, concrete/plaster wall, olive foliage, shelves out of focus, muted warm brown, moss green, cream palette, subtle film grain, honest architecture magazine photography. Right half has calm blurred wall and desk negative space for a UI panel overlay. Believable laptop and hands, no rendered graphics, no floating elements, no text, no logos, no neon, no exaggerated stock-photo smile, no tech fantasy. Image will accompany an animated agent product demonstration. Return the image and save it to a local file.
